## 📌 Project Overview

Analyzed e-commerce sales data using MySQL to generate insights into revenue, profit, customers, products, categories, and sales performance.

## 🗂️ Database Structure

* **Customers** – Customer details and locations
* **Products** – Product, category, price, and cost details
* **Orders** – Order dates, customers, and sales representatives
* **Order Details** – Product quantities and sales amounts
* **Sales Representatives** – Sales representative information

## 🛠️ SQL Skills Used

* SELECT, WHERE, ORDER BY & LIMIT
* Aggregate Functions, GROUP BY & HAVING
* INNER JOIN & CTEs
* CASE & COALESCE
* Subqueries
* Window Functions & Ranking
* LAG() & Cumulative Analysis
* Monthly & Category-wise Analysis

## 📊 Key Business Questions

* What are the top products and customers by revenue?
* Which categories generate the highest sales and profit?
* Which cities generate the most revenue?
* Which customers spend above average?
* What are the monthly revenue trends?
* Which products rank highest within each category?

## 🎯 Key Insights

Identified revenue and profit drivers, top-performing products and customers, category performance, city-wise sales, monthly trends, and customer spending patterns.
